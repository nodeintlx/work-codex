# NRG Bloom Information Core

Date: `2026-03-26`

This is the current information base NRG Bloom should build content around.

## The 4 Facts That Matter Most

1. NRG Bloom has real operating proof in Nigeria.
   The company has been operational at Ogboinbiri since February 2025 and internally
   describes the site as a `1.5 MW` operating deployment.

2. Compute is becoming more energy constrained, not less.
   IEA's 2025 analysis says electricity demand from data centres could more than
   double by 2030 to roughly `945 TWh`.

3. Nigeria's power problem is still severe enough to make distributed models matter.
   World Bank and related institutional materials still describe a massive electricity
   access deficit, large economic losses from unreliable supply, and a persistent gap
   between nominal and delivered power.

4. Flare-gas monetization in Nigeria is now legible enough to discuss as a market.
   NGFCP reporting, Green Flare's public project, and Crusoe's North American history
   together make the category easier for investors to understand.

## What These Facts Mean

- NRG Bloom should not sound like a company asking people to imagine a future.
- It should sound like a company already standing inside a market bottleneck.
- The bottleneck is not lack of demand for compute.
- The bottleneck is where reliable energy can actually be turned into compute.

## The Best Strategic Framing

### 1. Energy Delivery, Not Just Energy Supply

The strong argument is not simply that Africa has energy resources.
The stronger argument is that large amounts of value are trapped by delivery failure,
infrastructure gaps, transmission limits, flaring, and underused edge energy.

### 2. Operator Truth Over Macro Storytelling

NRG Bloom's strongest advantage is not that it can talk about Africa.
It is that it can connect macro infrastructure reality to field execution:

- modular deployment
- remote operations
- local complexity
- energy monetization
- real site proof

### 3. Category Creation Through Legibility

Investors do not fund obscure categories easily.
The content job is to make stranded-energy compute in Africa feel legible by connecting:

- institutional power-market evidence
- public flare-gas market structure
- known analogs like Crusoe
- NRG Bloom's own operating proof
