# NRG Bloom Content Intelligence

This folder is the information layer behind future content.

It is not designed to impress on its own. It is designed to make sure every future
post, memo, thread, or founder note starts from the right inputs:

- what is verified
- what matters most
- who needs to hear it
- what objection it answers
- what should not be said yet

## Core Files

- `information-core-2026-03-26.md`
  The shortest useful summary of what NRG Bloom should be saying now.
- `audience-priority-map.yaml`
  Who matters most, what they care about, and what proof moves them.
- `content-production-queue-2026-03-26.yaml`
  Ready-to-produce content angles mapped to audiences, facts, and objectives.
- `do-not-say-yet-2026-03-26.md`
  Claims that are not strong enough for public authority content yet.
- `raw-intake-template.md`
  The format to use when new facts, partnerships, or updates come in.

## Operating Rule

Every public-facing piece should be traceable to one of these buckets:

1. Operator proof
2. Market proof
3. Category proof
4. Strategic interpretation
5. Investor objection handling

If a piece cannot be traced back to one of those, it is probably filler.
