# Do Not Say Yet

These claims are not strong enough yet for public authority content.

## Hold For Better Proof

- `Built for $47K`
- `Heirs Energies commercial partnership under review`
- `Canurta as first public HPC client`
- `25% host-community power allocation`
- `Verra VM0029 / carbon-credit methodology as active company position`
- `$22B/year` Nigerian backup-generator spending
- `Africa has 211 data centers for 1.4 billion people`

## Why This Matters

These claims may be directionally true, partially true, or internally understood.
That is not enough.

The whole point of the current system is that NRG Bloom should start sounding more
serious than the average startup in this category. That means avoiding claims that can
be challenged easily.

## Safer Substitute Pattern

Instead of:
- exact unsupported numbers
- partner names that are not cleared
- climate claims that imply finalized methodology

Use:
- stronger verified operating proof
- clearer market-structure facts
- interpretation that does not overstate certainty
