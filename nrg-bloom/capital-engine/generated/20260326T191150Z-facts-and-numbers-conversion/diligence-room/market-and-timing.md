## nigeria_power_access_and_losses
Recent World Bank materials describe Nigeria as having the world's largest electricity access deficit, with roughly 85-90 million people lacking access and annual economic losses from poor power supply in roughly the $26-29 billion range.

Scope: external
Sources:
- [multilateral] World Bank press release — The World Bank Approves Additional Financing to Consolidate the Gains of Nigeria's Power Sector Recovery Program (https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2023/06/09/the-world-bank-approves-additional-financing-to-consolidate-the-gains-of-nigeria-s-power-sector-recovery-program)
  Excerpt: Lack of access to the electricity grid affects 45 percent of the population (90 million people), making Nigeria the country with the largest number of people not connected to electricity.
- [multilateral] World Bank DARES results brief — Nigeria Distributed Access through Renewable Energy Scale-up (DARES) Program-for-Results (https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099082124112015103/p17968714b12670871924f3b6c5d0ff4d8)
  Excerpt: About 85 million Nigerians do not have access to electricity and economic losses due to poor electricity supply are estimated at US$26.2 billion.
- [multilateral] World Bank PSRO press release — Nigeria to Keep the Lights on and Power its Economy (https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2020/06/23/nigeria-to-keep-the-lights-on-and-power-its-economy)
  Excerpt: About 47% of Nigerians do not have access to grid electricity... the economic cost of power shortages in Nigeria is estimated at around $28 billion.

Scope: external
Sources:
- [official_report] IEA Electricity 2025 — Electricity 2025 (https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-2025)
  Excerpt: Nigeria’s installed generation capacity rose to 14 GW in 2024, but average daily available capacity was 4.14 GW.
- [multilateral] World Bank project document — Nigeria Power Sector Guarantees Project (https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/193911617633470778/pdf/Nigeria-Power-Sector-Guarantees-Project.pdf)
  Excerpt: The electricity produced and distributed within Nigeria hovers between 3 GW and 4 GW, peaking at 5 GW.
- [multilateral] African Development Bank project document — Nigeria Electrification Project (https://www.afdb.org/fileadmin/uploads/afdb/Documents/Project-and-Operations/PESR_NG_NIGERIA_ELECTRIFICATION_PROJECT_CORR_EN-final.pdf)
