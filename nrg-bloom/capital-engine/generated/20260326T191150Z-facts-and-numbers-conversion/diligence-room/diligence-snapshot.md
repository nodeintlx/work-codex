# Diligence Snapshot

## Investor Questions and Evidence Paths

## Is this a real operating company or still a concept?
Lead with live site proof, current operating capacity, and modular deployment logic before any growth narrative.

- ogboinbiri_operational_since_2025_02: NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025.
- ogboinbiri_operational_capacity_1_5mw: NRG Bloom's Ogboinbiri site is described internally and on the investor page as 1.5 MW operational capacity.

## Why is this market worth attention now?
The opportunity is sharper because compute demand is becoming more energy constrained at the same time Nigeria's grid gap and digital-infrastructure gap remain visible.

- global_data_center_demand_doubles_2030: IEA analysis in 2025 said electricity demand from data centres worldwide is set to more than double by 2030 to around 945 TWh.
- nigeria_power_access_and_losses: Recent World Bank materials describe Nigeria as having the world's largest electricity access deficit, with roughly 85-90 million people lacking access and annual economic losses from poor power supply in roughly the $26-29 billion range.
- nigeria_data_center_capacity_pipeline_2025: 2025 market-intelligence coverage described Nigeria's third-party data-center market as still relatively small, with figures around 65.8 MW live and about 327.8 MW under construction or planned.

## Why should anyone believe flare-gas compute is financeable in Nigeria?
Because the category now has public regulatory structure, public local analogs, and a global precedent.

- ngfcp_permits_power_potential_2025: By December 2025, NGFCP reporting said 28 firms had final flare-gas permits across 49 awarded sites, with 42 bidders involved, 250-300 mmscfd of gas to be captured, and nearly 3 GW of power potential.
- green_flare_nigeria_53mw_2025: Green Flare announced a 53 MW flare-gas compute project in Nigeria in 2025, structured across three Delta State sites.
- crusoe_divestiture_2025: In March 2025, Crusoe said it sold its bitcoin-mining operation to NYDIG after deploying more than 425 modular data centers across seven U.S. states and Argentina.
