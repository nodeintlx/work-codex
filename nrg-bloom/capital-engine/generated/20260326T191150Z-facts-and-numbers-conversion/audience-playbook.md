# Audience Playbook

The goal is not broad attention. The goal is repeated exposure among people who can validate, fund, or accelerate the company.

## Energy and Infrastructure Capital
- Role in funnel: Primary capital
- Core thesis: NRG Bloom should look like a distributed infrastructure operator monetizing energy that the grid or market currently fails to use.
- Conversion goal: Advance to memo review and diligence call.
- Preferred channels: LinkedIn, Email, Warm intros
- Call to action: Review the investor memo and discuss deployment sequencing.
- Supporting proof:
  - NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025.
  - Recent World Bank materials describe Nigeria as having the world's largest electricity access deficit, with roughly 85-90 million people lacking access and annual economic losses from poor power supply in roughly the $26-29 billion range.
  - Recent institutional analyses describe Nigeria's grid as delivering only roughly 3-5 GW on average despite materially higher installed or nominal capacity.

## Compute and AI Investors
- Role in funnel: Primary capital
- Core thesis: The company should be framed as energy-edge compute infrastructure for a world where power availability is becoming a first-order constraint.
- Conversion goal: Advance to thesis discussion around where modular compute belongs in the stack.
- Preferred channels: X, LinkedIn, Email
- Call to action: Review the one-page brief and discuss how energy access shapes compute deployment.
- Supporting proof:
  - IEA analysis in 2025 said electricity demand from data centres worldwide is set to more than double by 2030 to around 945 TWh.
  - 2025 market-intelligence coverage described Nigeria's third-party data-center market as still relatively small, with figures around 65.8 MW live and about 327.8 MW under construction or planned.
  - NRG Bloom's Ogboinbiri site is described internally and on the investor page as 1.5 MW operational capacity.

## Africa-Focused Investors and DFIs
- Role in funnel: Primary capital
- Core thesis: NRG Bloom should be positioned as a company already operating in-market while most others are still explaining the opportunity from afar.
- Conversion goal: Advance to diligence around field credibility, regulatory fit, and expansion logic.
- Preferred channels: LinkedIn, Warm intros, Targeted outreach
- Call to action: Review the diligence snapshot and discuss the Nigeria expansion path.
- Supporting proof:
  - NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025.
  - By December 2025, NGFCP reporting said 28 firms had final flare-gas permits across 49 awarded sites, with 42 bidders involved, 250-300 mmscfd of gas to be captured, and nearly 3 GW of power potential.
  - Recent World Bank materials describe Nigeria as having the world's largest electricity access deficit, with roughly 85-90 million people lacking access and annual economic losses from poor power supply in roughly the $26-29 billion range.

## Industry Validators and Strategic Operators
- Role in funnel: Credibility
- Core thesis: Public validation from operators and market interpreters helps turn NRG Bloom from an interesting company into a reference point in the category.
- Conversion goal: Earn public validation, partner intros, and ecosystem credibility.
- Preferred channels: X, LinkedIn, Direct outreach
- Call to action: React publicly, comment, or make a relevant introduction.
- Supporting proof:
  - By December 2025, NGFCP reporting said 28 firms had final flare-gas permits across 49 awarded sites, with 42 bidders involved, 250-300 mmscfd of gas to be captured, and nearly 3 GW of power potential.
  - Green Flare announced a 53 MW flare-gas compute project in Nigeria in 2025, structured across three Delta State sites.
  - In March 2025, Crusoe said it sold its bitcoin-mining operation to NYDIG after deploying more than 425 modular data centers across seven U.S. states and Argentina.
