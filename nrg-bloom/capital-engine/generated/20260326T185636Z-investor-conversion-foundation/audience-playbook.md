# Audience Playbook

The goal is not broad attention. The goal is repeated exposure among people who can validate, fund, or accelerate the company.

## Infrastructure and Energy Funds
- Role in funnel: Primary capital
- Core thesis: NRG Bloom should look like a disciplined energy monetization platform with digital upside, not a speculative media company.
- Conversion goal: Advance to memo review and diligence call.
- Preferred channels: LinkedIn, Email, Warm intros
- Call to action: Review the investor memo and schedule a proof-of-execution call.
- Supporting proof:
  - NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025.
  - The World Bank's 2025 tracker said global gas flaring reached 151 bcm in 2024.
  - In December 2025, NUPRC said 28 firms received flare-gas permits under NGFCP, with 42 bidders awarded 49 flare sites.

## AI and Compute Investors
- Role in funnel: Primary capital
- Core thesis: The company should be framed as energy-edge compute infrastructure, with today’s economics grounded in real deployment logic rather than pure data-center speculation.
- Conversion goal: Advance to thesis discussion around energy-constrained compute.
- Preferred channels: X, LinkedIn, Email
- Call to action: Review the one-page brief and discuss the deployment model.
- Supporting proof:
  - NRG Bloom is a Montreal-based company operating infrastructure in Nigeria.
  - NRG Bloom's operating model is modular and containerized for remote stranded-energy deployment.
  - Africa's digital economy is widely projected to reach about $712 billion by 2050.

- Role in funnel: Credibility
- Core thesis: Their public validation helps investors treat NRG Bloom as a serious category operator.
- Conversion goal: Earn amplification, intros, and validation.
- Preferred channels: X, LinkedIn, Direct message
- Call to action: React publicly or introduce the company to relevant capital or partners.
- Supporting proof:
  - North Dakota paired large Bakken associated-gas output with stronger capture policy, reaching a 94% gross capture rate in July 2024 ahead of a 91% target effective January 1, 2025.
  - Texas and the Permian are the core U.S. associated-gas frontier: Texas produced record gas in 2024 and the Permian accounted for most U.S. production growth.
  - By March 2025, Crusoe said its flare-gas business had deployed more than 425 modular data centers across seven U.S. states including North Dakota and Texas.
