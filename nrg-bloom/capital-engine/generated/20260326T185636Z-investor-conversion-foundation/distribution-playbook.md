# Distribution Playbook

## Weekly Cadence

## Monday
- Asset: Founder post + quote card
- Audience: Infrastructure and Energy Funds
- Objective: Lead with proof of execution.
- Theme: Field proof before promises

## Tuesday
- Asset: X thread
- Audience: AI and Compute Investors
- Objective: Frame the market window.
- Theme: Why stranded-energy compute matters now

## Wednesday
- Asset: Carousel
- Audience: Africa-Focused Investors and DFIs
- Objective: Reduce frontier-market uncertainty.
- Theme: Montreal discipline, Nigerian field execution

## Thursday
- Asset: Short video or animated card
- Audience: Industry Validators and Strategic Operators
- Objective: Earn public validation and category reinforcement.
- Theme: North America analogs, Africa adaptation

## Friday
- Asset: Contrarian market note
- Audience: Analysts, Journalists, and Market Interpreters
- Objective: Own the category framing going into the next week.
- Theme: Why remote execution matters more than hyperscale rhetoric
