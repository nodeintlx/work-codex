# Diligence Snapshot

## Investor Questions and Evidence Paths

## Is this still a concept or is there real operating proof?
The company should answer this with site history first, not ambition. Investors should see operating proof before they see scale claims.

- ogboinbiri_operational_since_2025_02: NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025.
- montreal_based_nigeria_operator: NRG Bloom is a Montreal-based company operating infrastructure in Nigeria.

## Why this market now?
The market case improves when gas waste, digital demand, and regulatory movement are all visible at once.

- global_flaring_2024_151bcm: The World Bank's 2025 tracker said global gas flaring reached 151 bcm in 2024.
- africa_digital_economy_712b: Africa's digital economy is widely projected to reach about $712 billion by 2050.
- ngfcp_permits_2025: In December 2025, NUPRC said 28 firms received flare-gas permits under NGFCP, with 42 bidders awarded 49 flare sites.

## Why does the category itself deserve belief?
The category should not be sold as a brand-new idea. It should be shown as a proven logic already visible in other gas basins.

- north_dakota_bakken_capture: North Dakota paired large Bakken associated-gas output with stronger capture policy, reaching a 94% gross capture rate in July 2024 ahead of a 91% target effective January 1, 2025.
- texas_permian_scale: Texas and the Permian are the core U.S. associated-gas frontier: Texas produced record gas in 2024 and the Permian accounted for most U.S. production growth.
- crusoe_field_footprint_2025: By March 2025, Crusoe said its flare-gas business had deployed more than 425 modular data centers across seven U.S. states including North Dakota and Texas.

## Why can NRG Bloom specifically win?
The company wins if investors believe field execution, remote coordination, and modular deployment are harder to replicate than slideware.
