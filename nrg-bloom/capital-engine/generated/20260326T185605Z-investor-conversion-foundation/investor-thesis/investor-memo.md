# Investor Memo — NRG Bloom Investor Conversion Foundation

## Company Snapshot

- Company: NRG Bloom
- Headquarters: Montreal, Canada
- Operating geography: Nigeria, with category analogs benchmarked against North America
- Operating model: Modular, containerized remote compute infrastructure deployed against stranded or underutilized energy
- Current stage: Operating proof established; capital stack, market authority, and structured scale narrative now need to converge

## Positioning

NRG Bloom should present as a field-proven remote compute operator that turns stranded energy into monetizable digital infrastructure, not as a speculative Africa narrative.

## Core Investment Thesis

The company is most compelling when investors see a rare combination of real field execution in Nigeria, modular deployment logic, and a category that already has external proof in North American basins.

## Verified Proof Today

- NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025.
- NRG Bloom is a Montreal-based company operating infrastructure in Nigeria.
- NRG Bloom's operating model is modular and containerized for remote stranded-energy deployment.

## Why This Window Exists

Gas-utilization pressure, African digital-infrastructure demand, and global proof from stranded-gas compute analogs are all stronger than they were even one year ago. The opportunity is to fund the operator that can adapt that logic to African field conditions before the category narrative gets crowded.

- The World Bank's 2025 tracker said global gas flaring reached 151 bcm in 2024.
- Africa's digital economy is widely projected to reach about $712 billion by 2050.
- In December 2025, NUPRC said 28 firms received flare-gas permits under NGFCP, with 42 bidders awarded 49 flare sites.

## Category Lineage

NRG Bloom is not asking investors to believe stranded-gas compute is a speculative category. The category logic is already visible in North America; the question is which operator can adapt it to African field conditions.

- North Dakota paired large Bakken associated-gas output with stronger capture policy, reaching a 94% gross capture rate in July 2024 ahead of a 91% target effective January 1, 2025.
- Texas and the Permian are the core U.S. associated-gas frontier: Texas produced record gas in 2024 and the Permian accounted for most U.S. production growth.
- By March 2025, Crusoe said its flare-gas business had deployed more than 425 modular data centers across seven U.S. states including North Dakota and Texas.

## Why NRG Bloom Could Win

NRG Bloom can win if it keeps proving that remote operations discipline, local execution credibility, and modular deployment speed matter more than generic hyperscale storytelling.

## What Capital Unlocks

- Scale modular deployment capacity and shorten time from site approval to compute revenue.
- Strengthen field operations, monitoring, and logistics infrastructure across Nigeria.
- Expand commercial development with upstream gas counterparties and strategic partners.
- Turn a working remote-operations base into a repeatable expansion platform.

## What This Means For Investors

Best fit is investors who understand infrastructure execution risk, energy monetization, and why operational proof reduces frontier-market uncertainty.

## Next Diligence Step

Move qualified investors from social engagement into a memo review, then into a diligence call centered on proof of execution and deployment path.
