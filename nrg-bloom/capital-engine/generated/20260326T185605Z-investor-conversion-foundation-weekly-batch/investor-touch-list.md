# Investor Touch List

Use this alongside the weekly batch so every content publish has a matching investor follow-up motion.

## Infrastructure and Energy Funds
- Goal: Advance to memo review and diligence call.
- Trigger asset: founder proof post
- CTA: Review the investor memo and schedule a proof-of-execution call.

## AI and Compute Investors
- Goal: Advance to thesis discussion around energy-constrained compute.
- Trigger asset: market timing thread
- CTA: Review the one-page brief and discuss the deployment model.

## Africa-Focused Investors and DFIs
- Goal: Advance to diligence around local operating credibility and scale path.
- Trigger asset: operator truth quote card
- CTA: Review the diligence snapshot and discuss scale path in Nigeria.

## Industry Validators and Strategic Operators
- Goal: Earn amplification, intros, and validation.
- Trigger asset: North America analog post
- CTA: React publicly or introduce the company to relevant capital or partners.

## Analysts, Journalists, and Market Interpreters
- Goal: Earn citations, coverage, and category visibility.
- Trigger asset: timing and category lineage thread
- CTA: Use the company as a source or invite the founder into the conversation.
