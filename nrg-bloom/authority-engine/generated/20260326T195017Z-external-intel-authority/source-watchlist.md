# Source Watchlist

These are the sources that should keep feeding the authority system even when internal operating updates are limited.

## IEA
- Tier: institutional_primary
- Type: energy_and_compute
- Why it matters: Best source for the global energy-and-compute constraint story.
- Tracking focus: AI power demand, data centre demand, electricity outlook
- URL: https://www.iea.org

## World Bank
- Tier: institutional_primary
- Type: power_access_and_losses
- Why it matters: Best source for Nigerian electricity access, reliability, and economic-loss framing.
- Tracking focus: electricity access, economic losses, Nigeria power sector
- URL: https://www.worldbank.org

## African Development Bank
- Tier: institutional_primary
- Type: africa_infrastructure
- Why it matters: Useful for continent-scale infrastructure and capital framing.
- Tracking focus: digital economy, electrification, infrastructure finance
- URL: https://www.afdb.org

## NUPRC
- Tier: regulator
- Type: flare_gas_market
- Why it matters: Primary regulatory source for NGFCP and flare-gas commercialization.
- Tracking focus: NGFCP, flare permits, gas commercialization
- URL: https://www.nuprc.gov.ng

## EIA
- Tier: institutional_primary
- Type: north_america_analogs
- Why it matters: Best baseline for Texas, Permian, associated gas, and basin scale.
- Tracking focus: Texas gas, Permian growth, associated gas
- URL: https://www.eia.gov

## Arizton / market intelligence
- Tier: industry_analysis
- Type: data_center_market
- Why it matters: Useful for Nigeria data-center market sizing and pipeline framing.
- Tracking focus: Nigeria capacity, pipeline growth, market investment
- URL: https://www.arizton.com

## Crusoe
- Tier: company_signal
- Type: category_analog
- Why it matters: Public benchmark for flare-to-compute validation and category evolution.
- Tracking focus: AI pivot, modular deployments, flare mitigation history
- URL: https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom

## Green Flare
- Tier: company_signal
- Type: nigeria_analog
- Why it matters: Visible Nigerian analog that helps prove local market legibility.
- Tracking focus: project progress, MW announcements, Nigeria flare-to-compute
- URL: https://www.theenergymag.com/news/2025-05-28/green-flare-bitcoin-nigeria
