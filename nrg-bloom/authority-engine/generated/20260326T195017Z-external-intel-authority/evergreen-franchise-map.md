# Evergreen Franchise Map

These are the repeatable content franchises that should keep NRG Bloom visible.

## Market Intelligence
- Purpose: Turn trusted external developments into clear investor-relevant framing.
- Best audiences: energy infrastructure capital, compute and AI investors, analysts
- Tone: measured, data-first, non-hype
- Recommended formats: thread, market note, founder post
- Proof base:
  - IEA analysis in 2025 said electricity demand from data centres worldwide is set to more than double by 2030 to around 945 TWh.
  - Recent World Bank materials describe Nigeria as having the world's largest electricity access deficit, with roughly 85-90 million people lacking access and annual economic losses from poor power supply in roughly the $26-29 billion range.
  - Recent institutional analyses describe Nigeria's grid as delivering only roughly 3-5 GW on average despite materially higher installed or nominal capacity.
- Notes: This franchise should dominate during disclosure-constrained periods.

## Category Lineage
- Purpose: Make the category legible by connecting Nigeria to known analogs and public market moves.
- Best audiences: industry validators, investors, partners
- Tone: authoritative, comparative, operator-aware
- Recommended formats: LinkedIn company post, thread, video script
- Proof base:
  - By December 2025, NGFCP reporting said 28 firms had final flare-gas permits across 49 awarded sites, with 42 bidders involved, 250-300 mmscfd of gas to be captured, and nearly 3 GW of power potential.
  - Green Flare announced a 53 MW flare-gas compute project in Nigeria in 2025, structured across three Delta State sites.
  - In March 2025, Crusoe said it sold its bitcoin-mining operation to NYDIG after deploying more than 425 modular data centers across seven U.S. states and Argentina.
