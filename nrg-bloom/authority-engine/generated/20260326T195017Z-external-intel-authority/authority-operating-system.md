# Authority Operating System — NRG Bloom External Intelligence Authority System

This operating system is designed for periods when NRG Bloom should stay visible without depending on fresh operating disclosures.

## Core Principle

When fresh operating disclosure is constrained, NRG Bloom should stay visible by becoming the clearest interpreter of the stranded-energy compute category in Africa.

## Inputs

- Market signals from trusted external sources
- Category analogs from North America and Nigeria
- Sanitized founder insight that does not depend on live-sensitive details
- Investor objections that need repeated answers

## Constraints

- Do not rely on live TON-sensitive operating updates.
- Do not publish claims that require private commercial disclosure.
- Favor recurring authority over one-off hype.
- Use verified facts first, interpretation second.

## Weekly Lanes

## Monday
- Lane: market-intelligence
- Objective: Own the market bottleneck discussion early in the week.
- Output: Founder post or thread
- Primary objection: Why does this matter now?

## Wednesday
- Lane: category-lineage
- Objective: Make the category feel more legible than competitors make it.
- Output: LinkedIn company post or carousel
- Primary objection: Is this category real or still mostly narrative?

## Friday
- Lane: founder-conviction
- Objective: Keep founder voice present with sanitized insight.
- Output: Founder note or market interpretation
- Primary objection: Why should people listen to NRG Bloom?
