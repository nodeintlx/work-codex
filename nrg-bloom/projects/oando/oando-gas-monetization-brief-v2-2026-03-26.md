# NRG Bloom x Oando Energy Resources
## Gas Monetization Pilot Brief

**Prepared for:** Oando Energy Resources - Gas Business and Commercial Teams  
**Prepared by:** NRG Bloom Inc.  
**Date:** March 26, 2026  
**Status:** Draft V2 - gas-first commercial framing

---

## 1. Executive Summary

NRG Bloom proposes a pilot with Oando that converts stranded or underutilized gas into USD-denominated digital infrastructure revenue without requiring Oando to build or operate a data center platform.

The core idea is simple:

- Oando supplies gas or power from an approved site
- NRG Bloom funds, deploys, owns, and operates the modular digital infrastructure
- Oando earns new revenue from an asset stream that is currently under-monetized
- The structure can support Oando's flare reduction and gas monetization priorities without creating a new operational burden for Oando's core teams

NRG Bloom has already operated at Ogboinbiri since February 2025. This is not a speculative first entry into the Niger Delta environment. The pilot is intended to formalize and scale a model that has already been proven on the ground.

---

## 2. Why This Matters to Oando

This opportunity should be evaluated first as a gas monetization and site-efficiency project, not as a technology experiment.

For Oando, the value case is:

- monetize gas that currently produces limited or no direct revenue
- create a new USD-linked earnings stream from existing upstream operations
- advance flare reduction and emissions objectives
- preserve optionality across multiple sites without committing to a large initial capital program
- test a modular deployment model before portfolio-scale rollout

For NRG Bloom, the value case is:

- secure long-term gas or power access under a formalized structure
- deploy modular infrastructure at sites where the economics and operating conditions are already understood
- build a repeatable commercial template for wider rollout across Oando's portfolio

---

## 3. What NRG Bloom Brings

- Operational proof at Ogboinbiri since February 2025
- Local site experience in Bayelsa operating conditions
- Modular deployment model that can scale by site and gas availability
- Ability to finance and operate the digital infrastructure side of the project
- A structure designed to minimize execution burden on Oando

The important commercial point is that NRG Bloom is not asking Oando to become a data center operator. Oando remains the upstream asset owner and gas counterparty. NRG Bloom handles the infrastructure, operations, maintenance, and digital workload management.

## 4. Recommended Commercial Framing

We recommend that the next conversation focus on three commercial structures only.

### Structure A: Existing Power Monetization

Use existing excess electrical capacity at an Oando-controlled site where capacity is demonstrably available without disrupting existing obligations.

- Oando provides: excess electrical capacity
- NRG Bloom provides: modular digital infrastructure and full operations
- Oando capex: zero
- Oando revenue model: fixed power tariff or revenue share
- Best use case: fastest pilot where spare capacity already exists

### Structure B: Dedicated Gas-to-Power Pilot

Oando supports a dedicated pilot power arrangement for NRG Bloom's load at a selected site.

- Oando provides: gas supply and site support for dedicated generation
- NRG Bloom provides: digital infrastructure and operating layer
- Oando capex: limited or scoped depending on genset structure
- Oando revenue model: power sales and/or structured participation
- Best use case: clean pilot structure with clearer separation from existing site loads

### Structure C: Direct Gas Offtake Model

NRG Bloom purchases gas under a formal gas supply arrangement and deploys its own gas-to-power and digital infrastructure stack.

- Oando provides: gas supply from an approved flare or stranded-gas stream
- NRG Bloom provides: gas reception, generation, digital infrastructure, and operations
- Oando capex: zero
- Oando revenue model: gas sales or gas-linked pricing formula
- Best use case: most scalable structure for wider portfolio rollout

---

## 5. Commercial Principles

Before detailed pricing, the parties should align on five principles:

1. Oando should earn from gas that is currently under-monetized.
2. Oando should not be required to operate digital infrastructure.
3. The pilot should start at a site where approval and execution are most straightforward.
4. The commercial structure should be expandable to additional sites if the pilot succeeds.
5. Gas composition, flow data, and site constraints should drive final technical and pricing decisions.

---

## 6. Pilot Site Selection Logic

The initial pilot should prioritize a site with the following characteristics:

- clear Oando operational control
- available gas or excess power with minimal internal encumbrance
- manageable approval pathway
- acceptable security and logistics conditions
- sufficient site data to allow fast engineering validation

Where possible, the first pilot should avoid internal complexity that slows execution unnecessarily. The objective is to prove the revenue model quickly, then expand once the commercial and technical template is validated.

---

## 7. Information Required From Oando

To move from concept to a tailored commercial proposal, NRG Bloom would need:

- gas composition data
- flow-rate range and availability profile
- indication of whether the preferred path is existing power, dedicated generation, or direct gas offtake
- confirmation of candidate site or sites
- key internal constraints affecting approvals, site access, or infrastructure placement

Once this is provided, NRG Bloom can return with a tighter technical-commercial package aligned to Oando's preferred structure.

## 8. Proposed Next Step

NRG Bloom recommends a working session with Oando's Gas Business, Commercial, and relevant technical stakeholders to do the following:

1. select the preferred commercial structure
2. identify the most practical pilot site
3. confirm the minimum data package required for a tailored proposal
