objective here, so order and timing matter as much as the copy.

## Engagement tactics

1. **Quote-tweet the live pivot news within 60 minutes.** When CleanSpark, MARA, TeraWulf,
   Riot, Hut 8, or IREN announces an AI lease or a power acquisition, quote it with one line
   of NRG Bloom's read plus the Nigeria comparison, not a link to our thread. Example
   structure: "Another 300 MW repriced from hashrate to AI lease. Same arithmetic works on
   flare gas at roughly a fraction of the input cost. We have been running it since Feb
   2025." Save the thread link for the reply underneath.
2. **Reply with the proof-point, not the pitch, in AI-datacenter-power threads.** When large
   accounts argue about interconnection queues, grid constraints, or where the next
   gigawatt comes from, reply with the single Nigeria fact that reframes it (14 GW installed
   vs roughly 4 GW delivered, or 49 auctioned flare sites with close to 3 GW of potential).
   One fact, no CTA. Replies to big accounts outperform original posts for cold-start reach.
3. **Green Flare specifically is the highest-leverage adjacency.** Any Green Flare, NUPRC, or
   NGFCP news is a chance to be the operator voice in a Nigerian story that Western compute
   accounts are already curious about. Support them publicly; a second credible Nigerian
   operator makes the category legible, which helps us more than it costs us.

## Guardrails

Nothing in this sprint may drift into the `do-not-say-yet` list: no build-cost figure, no
partner names, no host-community allocation percentage, no carbon-methodology position. If
a reply thread pulls toward any of those, answer with operating proof instead.
