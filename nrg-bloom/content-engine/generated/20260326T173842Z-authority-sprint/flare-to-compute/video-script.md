# Short Video Script
**Title:** Stop Treating Gas Flaring as an ESG Problem Only
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
Gas flaring is usually framed as an ESG problem. That framing is too small.

## Body
Gas flaring is not just an emissions line item. It is stranded monetization capacity waiting for the right operating model.

For operators willing to pair remote generation with modular compute, flare gas becomes both a methane-reduction lever and a revenue engine.

First: A flare site can be reframed from penalty exposure into a site-level monetization asset.

Second: Modular compute creates an offtake path where pipelines, grid upgrades, or industrial load are too slow or too expensive.

Third: The best projects are not climate theater. They are hard-nosed operating businesses that happen to reduce emissions.

The real breakthrough is not carbon accounting. It is making methane mitigation economically irresistible.

## Close
The moment stranded gas earns more in compute than it costs in penalties, the conversation changes.
