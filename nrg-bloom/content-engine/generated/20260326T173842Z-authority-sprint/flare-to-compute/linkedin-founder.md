# LinkedIn Founder Post
**Title:** Stop Treating Gas Flaring as an ESG Problem Only
**Channel:** Founder POV
**Pillar:** stranded-energy

The biggest mistake in gas-flaring conversations is treating the flare only as a compliance problem.

Gas flaring is not just an emissions line item. It is stranded monetization capacity waiting for the right operating model.

That is not just a talking point.
It points to the deeper truth: For operators willing to pair remote generation with modular compute, flare gas becomes both a methane-reduction lever and a revenue engine.

Here is what operators see that commentators miss:

- A flare site can be reframed from penalty exposure into a site-level monetization asset.
- Modular compute creates an offtake path where pipelines, grid upgrades, or industrial load are too slow or too expensive.
- The best projects are not climate theater. They are hard-nosed operating businesses that happen to reduce emissions.

The real breakthrough is not carbon accounting. It is making methane mitigation economically irresistible.

That is why we think the next generation of African compute will not be won by whoever has the biggest slide deck.
It will be won by whoever can finance, deploy, and operate remote infrastructure with discipline in the field.

NRG Bloom is focused on the infrastructure layer that makes that shift executable.

What would happen if flare-reduction projects were evaluated first by field economics and only second by sustainability reporting?
