# Short Video Script
**Title:** Speed Is Not a Feature in African Compute. It Is a Survival Trait.
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
If your African data-center plan takes two years to prove, you probably do not have an African data-center plan.

## Body
A two-year data-center timeline is not just slow in frontier markets. It is often structurally wrong.

Containerized deployment matters because energy access windows, site politics, logistics, and partner timelines move faster than conventional campus development assumptions.

First: A modular deployment can prove value before a conventional project would even finish its permitting deck.

Second: Portable infrastructure changes negotiating leverage because the asset is not trapped to one site on day one.

Third: In emerging-market infrastructure, speed compounds into trust, proof, and better capital efficiency.

The future of frontier compute will be built by teams that think like expeditionary infrastructure companies, not suburban real-estate developers.

## Close
Modular speed is not cosmetic. It is the difference between momentum and drift.
