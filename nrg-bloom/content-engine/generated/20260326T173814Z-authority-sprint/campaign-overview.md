# NRG Bloom Authority Sprint

Brand: NRG Bloom
Tagline: Remote compute infrastructure on stranded energy

## Positioning

NRG Bloom is not a generic data-center company. It is a remote infrastructure operator turning stranded African energy into sovereign compute capacity.

## Editorial Thesis

The market is crowded with commentary about AI, Bitcoin, and Africa. The opening is to sound like the company that has actually run hardware in the field, understands site reality, and can explain the economic logic better than both energy insiders and crypto tourists.
