# LinkedIn Company Post
**Title:** The Bakken Was a Proving Ground, Not a Footnote
**Channel:** Company Page

The Bakken should not be treated as a historical curiosity in flare-gas compute. It is one of the places where the operating model was stress-tested.

At NRG Bloom, we are building around a simple operating belief: North Dakota matters because it combined associated-gas scale, hard capture targets, and real infrastructure build-out under pressure.

Our current view:

- North Dakota's policy framework kept pushing gas capture higher while production stayed large.
- That is exactly the kind of environment where flare-to-compute solutions either prove themselves or get exposed.
- NRG Bloom should speak about African deployment with the same seriousness that Bakken operators brought to gas handling and utilization.

NRG Bloom is studying the Bakken as an operating blueprint, not just as industry trivia.
