If You Want Scale, You Have to Understand Texas

This piece is built for strong short-form retention. It uses Texas and the Permian as the scale analog, then pivots to the argument that African field execution requires a different operator muscle.

Core Line
If your stranded-gas thesis has no view on Texas, it probably has no view on scale. Know Texas for scale. Know Africa for execution.

Texas produced record natural gas volumes in 2024, and the Permian drove most U.S. production growth.

That is what makes Texas useful in the narrative. It is not there for spectacle. It is there to anchor the audience in basin-scale reality before NRG Bloom pivots to the harder question of execution.

The strategic point is that scale and execution are different disciplines. Texas teaches where volume is concentrated. Africa teaches what it takes to deploy, operate, and recover in environments where the field reality is less forgiving.

That contrast is where NRG Bloom should live. Not pretending the markets are identical, but showing it understands what each one teaches.
