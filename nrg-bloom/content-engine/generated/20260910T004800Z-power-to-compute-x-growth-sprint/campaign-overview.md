# NRG Bloom Power-to-Compute X Growth Sprint

Brand: NRG Bloom
Tagline: Remote compute infrastructure on stranded energy

## Positioning

X-first sprint built for reach, not for a pitch deck. Every brief ships as a thread and every thread rides the live 2026 conversation about Bitcoin miners becoming AI compute landlords, with Nigeria as the proof case nobody in that conversation is talking about yet.

## Editorial Thesis

The power-to-compute story is already being told loudly by US-listed miners and sell-side analysts. NRG Bloom's opening is not to repeat it. It is to point out that the entire argument, once you strip out the American grid, describes the Niger Delta better than it describes Texas, and to say that from a site that has been running since February 2025.
