# Short Video Script
**Title:** Nigeria Turned Flared Gas Into a Permit Market. Compute Barely Noticed.
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
Nigeria auctioned 49 flare sites. The AI industry did not notice.

## Body
28 firms hold final NGFCP permits across 49 awarded flare sites, covering 250 to 300 mmscfd of gas and close to 3 GW of power potential.

Most people hear that and think market size.
The more useful read is this:

Flare-gas compute in Nigeria is not a pilot idea anymore. It is an auctioned, permitted market with named counterparties and a regulator that publishes the numbers.

First: NUPRC ran an auction. 42 bidders won 49 sites. In December 2025 it issued permits to 28 firms. That is procurement, not speculation.

Second: The world flared 151 billion cubic meters of gas in 2024, the highest level since 2007. Nigeria is a significant contributor, and those molecules are already at surface.

Third: Flare gas is the one fuel where the alternative use is combustion into the sky. That is what makes the delivered power cost structurally low rather than temporarily low.

Everyone chasing AI power is fighting over queue positions on congested grids. The uncontested megawatts are in the basins nobody wants to fly to.

## Close
Permitted gas, already burning, close to three gigawatts of potential. That is a market, not a thesis.
