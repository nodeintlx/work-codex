# LinkedIn Company Post
**Title:** Nigeria Turned Flared Gas Into a Permit Market. Compute Barely Noticed.
**Channel:** Company Page

There is a country where close to 3 GW of power is sitting inside permitted, already-burning gas, and the AI buildout is not even looking at it.

28 firms hold final NGFCP permits across 49 awarded flare sites, covering 250 to 300 mmscfd of gas and close to 3 GW of power potential.

Flare-gas compute in Nigeria is not a pilot idea anymore. It is an auctioned, permitted market with named counterparties and a regulator that publishes the numbers.

The more useful read is this:

- NUPRC ran an auction. 42 bidders won 49 sites. In December 2025 it issued permits to 28 firms. That is procurement, not speculation.
- The world flared 151 billion cubic meters of gas in 2024, the highest level since 2007. Nigeria is a significant contributor, and those molecules are already at surface.
- Flare gas is the one fuel where the alternative use is combustion into the sky. That is what makes the delivered power cost structurally low rather than temporarily low.

Everyone chasing AI power is fighting over queue positions on congested grids. The uncontested megawatts are in the basins nobody wants to fly to.

NRG Bloom builds containerized compute at the flare, in Bayelsa State, and has been doing it since February 2025.
