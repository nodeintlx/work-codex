# Data Sheet — Nigeria Turned Flared Gas Into a Permit Market. Compute Barely Noticed.

Brief slug: ngfcp-permit-market
Hero stat: 28 firms hold final NGFCP permits across 49 awarded flare sites, covering 250 to 300 mmscfd of gas and close to 3 GW of power potential.
Core thesis: Flare-gas compute in Nigeria is not a pilot idea anymore. It is an auctioned, permitted market with named counterparties and a regulator that publishes the numbers.

## Claims Used In The Post

- Hook claim: There is a country where close to 3 GW of power is sitting inside permitted, already-burning gas, and the AI buildout is not even looking at it.
- Proof point 1: NUPRC ran an auction. 42 bidders won 49 sites. In December 2025 it issued permits to 28 firms. That is procurement, not speculation.
- Proof point 2: The world flared 151 billion cubic meters of gas in 2024, the highest level since 2007. Nigeria is a significant contributor, and those molecules are already at surface.
- Proof point 3: Flare gas is the one fuel where the alternative use is combustion into the sky. That is what makes the delivered power cost structurally low rather than temporarily low.
- Contrarian take: Everyone chasing AI power is fighting over queue positions on congested grids. The uncontested megawatts are in the basins nobody wants to fly to.
