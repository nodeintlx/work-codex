# Data Sheet — Bitcoin Mining Is the On-Ramp. It Was Never the Destination.

Brief slug: bitcoin-to-ai-onramp
Hero stat: 53 MW of Nigerian flare-gas capacity was announced in 2025 with the sequence stated out loud: Bitcoin first because it deploys fastest, then AI compute and cloud on the same power.
Core thesis: Bitcoin is the only compute workload that will move to the power instead of demanding the power move to it, which makes it the cheapest way on earth to commission a remote site.

## Claims Used In The Post

- Hook claim: Every serious stranded-power operator runs the same sequence. Mine Bitcoin to prove the megawatts are real. Then rent those megawatts to AI. Nigeria started that sequence early.
- Proof point 1: Green Flare said it at announcement: Bitcoin first because it is easiest to deploy and monetise, then AI compute and cloud services on the same flare gas.
- Proof point 2: TeraWulf proved the back half of that sequence in Q1 2026, when HPC lease revenue passed mining revenue at the company for the first time.
- Proof point 3: Crusoe walked the whole arc: more than 425 modular data centers deployed on stranded gas, then a sale of the mining business to focus on AI infrastructure.
- Contrarian take: Calling this a pivot misreads it. Mining is not being abandoned. It is being used as the commissioning load that makes a remote site bankable enough to host anything else.
