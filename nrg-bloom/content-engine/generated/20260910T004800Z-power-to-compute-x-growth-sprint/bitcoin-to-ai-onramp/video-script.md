# Short Video Script
**Title:** Bitcoin Mining Is the On-Ramp. It Was Never the Destination.
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
Mining was never the destination. It is how you prove the megawatts are real.

## Body
53 MW of Nigerian flare-gas capacity was announced in 2025 with the sequence stated out loud: Bitcoin first because it deploys fastest, then AI compute and cloud on the same power.

Most people hear that and think market size.
The operating takeaway is straightforward:

Bitcoin is the only compute workload that will move to the power instead of demanding the power move to it, which makes it the cheapest way on earth to commission a remote site.

First: Green Flare said it at announcement: Bitcoin first because it is easiest to deploy and monetise, then AI compute and cloud services on the same flare gas.

Second: TeraWulf proved the back half of that sequence in Q1 2026, when HPC lease revenue passed mining revenue at the company for the first time.

Third: Crusoe walked the whole arc: more than 425 modular data centers deployed on stranded gas, then a sale of the mining business to focus on AI infrastructure.

Calling this a pivot misreads it. Mining is not being abandoned. It is being used as the commissioning load that makes a remote site bankable enough to host anything else.

## Close
Same site, same containers, different tenant. That is the whole thesis.
