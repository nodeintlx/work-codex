# X Thread

1/ Every serious stranded-power operator runs the same sequence. Mine Bitcoin to prove the megawatts are real. Then rent those megawatts to AI. Nigeria started that sequence early.

2/ 53 MW of Nigerian flare-gas capacity was announced in 2025 with the sequence stated out loud: Bitcoin first because it deploys fastest, then AI compute and cloud on the same power.

3/ This is where the market still gets lazy. Bitcoin is the only compute workload that will move to the power instead of demanding the power move to it, which makes it the cheapest way on earth to commission a remote site.

4/ Green Flare said it at announcement: Bitcoin first because it is easiest to deploy and monetise, then AI compute and cloud services on the same flare gas.

5/ TeraWulf proved the back half of that sequence in Q1 2026, when HPC lease revenue passed mining revenue at the company for the first time.

6/ Crusoe walked the whole arc: more than 425 modular data centers deployed on stranded gas, then a sale of the mining business to focus on AI infrastructure.

7/ Calling this a pivot misreads it. Mining is not being abandoned. It is being used as the commissioning load that makes a remote site bankable enough to host anything else.

8/ NRG Bloom is on that same arc in the Niger Delta, with containers designed to change tenants without changing sites.

9/ Is Bitcoin mining best understood as an industry, or as the commissioning load for every megawatt too remote for a grid customer?
