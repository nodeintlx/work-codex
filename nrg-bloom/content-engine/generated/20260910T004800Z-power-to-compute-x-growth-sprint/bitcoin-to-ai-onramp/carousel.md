# Carousel Brief — Bitcoin Mining Is the On-Ramp. It Was Never the Destination.

## Slide 1
- Heading: Bitcoin Mining Is the On-Ramp. It Was Never the Destination.
- Body: Every serious stranded-power operator runs the same sequence. Mine Bitcoin to prove the megawatts are real. Then rent those megawatts to AI. Nigeria started that sequence early.

## Slide 2
- Heading: The Stat
- Body: 53 MW of Nigerian flare-gas capacity was announced in 2025 with the sequence stated out loud: Bitcoin first because it deploys fastest, then AI compute and cloud on the same power.

## Slide 3
- Heading: What Most People Miss
- Body: Bitcoin is the only compute workload that will move to the power instead of demanding the power move to it, which makes it the cheapest way on earth to commission a remote site.

## Slide 4
- Heading: Operator Truth #1
- Body: Green Flare said it at announcement: Bitcoin first because it is easiest to deploy and monetise, then AI compute and cloud services on the same flare gas.

## Slide 5
- Heading: Operator Truth #2
- Body: TeraWulf proved the back half of that sequence in Q1 2026, when HPC lease revenue passed mining revenue at the company for the first time.

## Slide 6
- Heading: Operator Truth #3
- Body: Crusoe walked the whole arc: more than 425 modular data centers deployed on stranded gas, then a sale of the mining business to focus on AI infrastructure.

## Slide 7
- Heading: The Contrarian Take
- Body: Calling this a pivot misreads it. Mining is not being abandoned. It is being used as the commissioning load that makes a remote site bankable enough to host anything else.

## Slide 8
- Heading: Call To Action
- Body: NRG Bloom is on that same arc in the Niger Delta, with containers designed to change tenants without changing sites.
