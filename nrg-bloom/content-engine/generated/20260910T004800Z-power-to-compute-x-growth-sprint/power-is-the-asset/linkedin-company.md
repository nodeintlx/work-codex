# LinkedIn Company Post
**Title:** Power Is the Asset. Compute Is Just the Tenant.
**Channel:** Company Page

Bitcoin mining was never the business. Owning power was the business. 2026 is the year the market finally priced it that way.

$21 million from compute leases against $13 million from mining. That was TeraWulf's Q1 2026, and it was the first quarter hosting outearned hashrate.

Miners are not becoming AI companies. They are being revalued as power companies that happened to solve interconnection, land, and cooling years before anyone needed them to.

What matters underneath the headline is simpler:

- Morgan Stanley now describes miners as owners of powered shell assets: grid connections, land, and development pipelines that can carry long-term AI leases.
- CleanSpark said in January 2026 it was assembling more than 890 MW of potential utility capacity in Texas specifically for AI and high-performance computing workloads.
- None of that logic is uniquely American. It works anywhere a company controls cheap power and can put a container next to it, which is what NRG Bloom has been doing in the Niger Delta since February 2025.

The scarce input in AI is not chips. It is energized land with a power source and somebody willing to operate it. Nigeria has a lot of the first and very little of the third.

NRG Bloom has been converting stranded Niger Delta gas into compute since February 2025. Same playbook, earlier in the curve, cheaper input.
