# Data Sheet — Power Is the Asset. Compute Is Just the Tenant.

Brief slug: power-is-the-asset
Hero stat: $21 million from compute leases against $13 million from mining. That was TeraWulf's Q1 2026, and it was the first quarter hosting outearned hashrate.
Core thesis: Miners are not becoming AI companies. They are being revalued as power companies that happened to solve interconnection, land, and cooling years before anyone needed them to.

## Claims Used In The Post

- Hook claim: Bitcoin mining was never the business. Owning power was the business. 2026 is the year the market finally priced it that way.
- Proof point 1: Morgan Stanley now describes miners as owners of powered shell assets: grid connections, land, and development pipelines that can carry long-term AI leases.
- Proof point 2: CleanSpark said in January 2026 it was assembling more than 890 MW of potential utility capacity in Texas specifically for AI and high-performance computing workloads.
- Proof point 3: None of that logic is uniquely American. It works anywhere a company controls cheap power and can put a container next to it, which is what NRG Bloom has been doing in the Niger Delta since February 2025.
- Contrarian take: The scarce input in AI is not chips. It is energized land with a power source and somebody willing to operate it. Nigeria has a lot of the first and very little of the third.
