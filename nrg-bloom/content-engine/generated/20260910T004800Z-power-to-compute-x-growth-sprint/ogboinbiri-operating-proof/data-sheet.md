# Data Sheet — Everyone Has a 100 MW Announcement. Very Few Have a Running Site.

Brief slug: ogboinbiri-operating-proof
Hero stat: 1.5 MW operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025. Not announced, not permitted, not planned. Running.
Core thesis: In frontier power, the only number that predicts whether you can build the next 50 MW is whether you already survived the first one.

## Claims Used In The Post

- Hook claim: Everyone in this category has a 100 MW announcement. Almost nobody has a site where a human being has to physically show up on a Sunday because a genset tripped.
- Proof point 1: NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025, at 1.5 MW.
- Proof point 2: The model is containerized on purpose. Remote Niger Delta sites do not forgive stick-built construction schedules.
- Proof point 3: Nigeria's whole third-party data center base was reported around 65.8 MW live with roughly 327.8 MW in the pipeline. The market is still small enough that operating proof is a real edge.
- Contrarian take: Announced capacity in this sector is a marketing number. Energized capacity is a competence number. The distance between the two is where most of these projects quietly die.
