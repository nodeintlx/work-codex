# Short Video Script
**Title:** Everyone Has a 100 MW Announcement. Very Few Have a Running Site.
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
Everyone has a 100 MW announcement. Very few have a running site.

## Body
1.5 MW operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025. Not announced, not permitted, not planned. Running.

Most people hear that and think market size.
The operating takeaway is straightforward:

In frontier power, the only number that predicts whether you can build the next 50 MW is whether you already survived the first one.

First: NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025, at 1.5 MW.

Second: The model is containerized on purpose. Remote Niger Delta sites do not forgive stick-built construction schedules.

Third: Nigeria's whole third-party data center base was reported around 65.8 MW live with roughly 327.8 MW in the pipeline. The market is still small enough that operating proof is a real edge.

Announced capacity in this sector is a marketing number. Energized capacity is a competence number. The distance between the two is where most of these projects quietly die.

## Close
Energized beats announced. Every single time.
