# Carousel Brief — Everyone Has a 100 MW Announcement. Very Few Have a Running Site.

## Slide 1
- Heading: Everyone Has a 100 MW Announcement. Very Few Have a Running Site.
- Body: Everyone in this category has a 100 MW announcement. Almost nobody has a site where a human being has to physically show up on a Sunday because a genset tripped.

## Slide 2
- Heading: The Stat
- Body: 1.5 MW operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025. Not announced, not permitted, not planned. Running.

## Slide 3
- Heading: What Most People Miss
- Body: In frontier power, the only number that predicts whether you can build the next 50 MW is whether you already survived the first one.

## Slide 4
- Heading: Operator Truth #1
- Body: NRG Bloom has been operational at Ogboinbiri in Bayelsa State since February 2025, at 1.5 MW.

## Slide 5
- Heading: Operator Truth #2
- Body: The model is containerized on purpose. Remote Niger Delta sites do not forgive stick-built construction schedules.

## Slide 6
- Heading: Operator Truth #3
- Body: Nigeria's whole third-party data center base was reported around 65.8 MW live with roughly 327.8 MW in the pipeline. The market is still small enough that operating proof is a real edge.

## Slide 7
- Heading: The Contrarian Take
- Body: Announced capacity in this sector is a marketing number. Energized capacity is a competence number. The distance between the two is where most of these projects quietly die.

## Slide 8
- Heading: Call To Action
- Body: If you want to know whether an operator can handle the Niger Delta, ask what broke in month four and who fixed it.
