# Carousel Brief — Nigeria Is Growing Data Centers, But the Market Is Still Small Enough for Edge Operators to Matter

## Slide 1
- Heading: Nigeria Is Growing Data Centers, But the Market Is Still Small Enough for Edge Operators to Matter
- Body: Nigeria's data-centre story is real, but the more interesting point is how early the market still is.

## Slide 2
- Heading: The Stat
- Body: Market coverage in 2025 said Nigeria had about 65.8 MW of third-party data-centre capacity and about 327.8 MW more in the pipeline.

## Slide 3
- Heading: What Most People Miss
- Body: The market is no longer empty, but it is still small enough that modular edge operators with real site access can create meaningful infrastructure before the landscape hardens.

## Slide 4
- Heading: Operator Truth #1
- Body: The capacity that exists today is still small relative to the country's population, digital demand, and power constraints.

## Slide 5
- Heading: Operator Truth #2
- Body: Pipeline growth is real, but large centralized capacity does not eliminate the need for distributed energy-edge infrastructure.

## Slide 6
- Heading: Operator Truth #3
- Body: NRG Bloom's 1.5 MW operating proof matters because this category will not be won only by the biggest campus. It will also be won by operators who can monetize constrained or stranded energy where it already exists.

## Slide 7
- Heading: The Contrarian Take
- Body: The mistake is assuming the whole market resolves into hyperscale campuses. Nigeria is still early enough for smaller, disciplined operators to define valuable pieces of the stack.

## Slide 8
- Heading: Call To Action
- Body: NRG Bloom is building a voice around the part of the digital-infrastructure market where energy realism and modular execution still matter.
