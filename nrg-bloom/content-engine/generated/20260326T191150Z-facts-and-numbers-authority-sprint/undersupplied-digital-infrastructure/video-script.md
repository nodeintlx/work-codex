# Short Video Script
**Title:** Nigeria Is Growing Data Centers, But the Market Is Still Small Enough for Edge Operators to Matter
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
Nigeria's data-centre market is growing, but it is still early enough for edge operators to matter.

## Body
Market coverage in 2025 said Nigeria had about 65.8 MW of third-party data-centre capacity and about 327.8 MW more in the pipeline.

The market is no longer empty, but it is still small enough that modular edge operators with real site access can create meaningful infrastructure before the landscape hardens.

First: The capacity that exists today is still small relative to the country's population, digital demand, and power constraints.

Second: Pipeline growth is real, but large centralized capacity does not eliminate the need for distributed energy-edge infrastructure.

Third: NRG Bloom's 1.5 MW operating proof matters because this category will not be won only by the biggest campus. It will also be won by operators who can monetize constrained or stranded energy where it already exists.

The mistake is assuming the whole market resolves into hyperscale campuses. Nigeria is still early enough for smaller, disciplined operators to define valuable pieces of the stack.

## Close
The companies that understand both centralized demand and edge energy constraints will shape what gets built next.
