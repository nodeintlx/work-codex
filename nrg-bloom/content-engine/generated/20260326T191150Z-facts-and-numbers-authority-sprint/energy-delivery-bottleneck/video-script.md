# Short Video Script
**Title:** The Constraint Is Not Demand. It Is Energy Delivery.
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
If your compute thesis ignores energy delivery, it is missing the actual bottleneck.

## Body
IEA says data-centre electricity demand could more than double by 2030, while World Bank materials still describe Nigeria as having the world's largest electricity-access deficit.

The real bottleneck is whether energy can be delivered where compute can actually use it. That is why remote energy-edge infrastructure matters.

First: The global compute market is moving into a period where electricity availability matters more, not less.

Second: Nigeria still combines a massive electricity-access gap with a persistent grid delivery gap and large economic losses from unreliable power.

Third: NRG Bloom's advantage is not abstract optimism about Africa. It is operating a modular compute model at the point where wasted or trapped energy can be monetized.

The market does not need another generic Africa data-centre narrative. It needs operators who understand that energy delivery, not demand slides, decides what gets built.

## Close
The next infrastructure winners will be the teams that know where energy can really be turned into compute.
