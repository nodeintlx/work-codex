# Short Video Script
**Title:** NGFCP Turned Flare Monetization Into a Governed Market
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
NGFCP changed the conversation. This is no longer a vague flare-gas story.

## Body
By December 2025, NGFCP reporting pointed to 28 final permits, 49 awarded sites, and nearly 3 GW of power potential.

The category is moving from theory into a governed market. NGFCP made that visible, Green Flare proved other players are mobilizing, and Crusoe's North America arc proved the model itself was never imaginary.

First: NUPRC's permit process gives flare monetization a more legible commercial frame than most outsiders realize.

Second: Green Flare's 53 MW announcement shows the Nigerian market is already attracting public flare-to-compute build-outs.

Third: Crusoe's 2025 divestiture matters because it confirms the model was proven strongly enough to become part of a larger power-and-compute consolidation story.

The most interesting question is no longer whether flare-to-compute is real. It is who will build the African operators investors trust once the category becomes legible.

## Close
The market is becoming legible. The next question is who earns the right to lead it.
