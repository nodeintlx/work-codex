# Short Video Script
**Title:** NGFCP Turned Flare Monetization Into a Governed Market
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
NGFCP changed the conversation. This is no longer a vague flare-gas story.

## Body
By December 2025, NGFCP reporting pointed to 28 final permits, 49 awarded sites, and nearly 3 GW of power potential.

Most people hear that and think market size.
The more useful read is this:

This is what category formation looks like: regulation, public projects, and analogs that make the market easier to underwrite.

First: NGFCP gives flare monetization a more legible commercial frame than most outsiders realize.

Second: Green Flare's 53 MW announcement shows the Nigerian market is already attracting visible build-outs.

Third: Crusoe's exit matters because it shows the model was real enough to become part of a larger power-and-compute story.

The interesting question is not whether flare-to-compute is real. It is which African operators investors will trust once the market becomes legible.

## Close
The market is getting easier to read. The next question is who earns the right to lead it.
