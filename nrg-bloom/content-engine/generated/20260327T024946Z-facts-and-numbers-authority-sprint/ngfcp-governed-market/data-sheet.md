# Data Sheet — NGFCP Turned Flare Monetization Into a Governed Market

Brief slug: ngfcp-governed-market
Hero stat: By December 2025, NGFCP reporting pointed to 28 final permits, 49 awarded sites, and nearly 3 GW of power potential.
Core thesis: This is what category formation looks like: regulation, public projects, and analogs that make the market easier to underwrite.

## Claims Used In The Post

- Hook claim: Flare-gas compute in Nigeria should not be framed like a fringe experiment anymore.
- Proof point 1: NGFCP gives flare monetization a more legible commercial frame than most outsiders realize.
- Proof point 2: Green Flare's 53 MW announcement shows the Nigerian market is already attracting visible build-outs.
- Proof point 3: Crusoe's exit matters because it shows the model was real enough to become part of a larger power-and-compute story.
- Contrarian take: The interesting question is not whether flare-to-compute is real. It is which African operators investors will trust once the market becomes legible.
