# Carousel Brief — Nigeria Is Growing Data Centers, But the Market Is Still Small Enough for Edge Operators to Matter

## Slide 1
- Heading: Nigeria Is Growing Data Centers, But the Market Is Still Small Enough for Edge Operators to Matter
- Body: Nigeria's data-centre story is real. The more useful point is how early the market still is.

## Slide 2
- Heading: The Stat
- Body: Market coverage in 2025 said Nigeria had about 65.8 MW of third-party data-centre capacity and about 327.8 MW more in the pipeline.

## Slide 3
- Heading: What Most People Miss
- Body: The market is no longer empty, but it is still early enough for disciplined edge operators to matter.

## Slide 4
- Heading: Operator Truth #1
- Body: The capacity online today is still small relative to the country's digital demand and power constraints.

## Slide 5
- Heading: Operator Truth #2
- Body: Pipeline growth is real, but more campuses do not eliminate stranded-energy problems.

## Slide 6
- Heading: Operator Truth #3
- Body: NRG Bloom's 1.5 MW operating proof matters because this market will not be won only by the biggest site. It will also be won by operators who can deploy where energy already exists.

## Slide 7
- Heading: The Contrarian Take
- Body: The mistake is assuming the whole market resolves into hyperscale campuses. Early markets usually reward the teams that solve the awkward parts first.
