# Short Video Script
**Title:** From Montreal to the Niger Delta
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
Montreal to Bayelsa is not a slogan. It is an operating system.

## Body
NRG Bloom is a Montreal-based company operating infrastructure in Nigeria.

Cross-border execution is a real moat when a team can coordinate Canadian management discipline with Nigerian field reality.

First: NRG Bloom's records tie Montreal headquarters directly to an active Nigerian operating footprint.

Second: Remote infrastructure management demands process, trust, and constant on-the-ground coordination.

Third: The companies that bridge continents well build more than projects. They build execution credibility.

Distance is not the weakness. Lack of operating discipline is.

## Close
Cross-border infrastructure works when discipline travels with the company.
