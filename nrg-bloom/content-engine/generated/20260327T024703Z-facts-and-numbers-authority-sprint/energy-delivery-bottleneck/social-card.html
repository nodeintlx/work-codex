The Constraint Is Not Demand. It Is Energy Delivery.

IEA says data-centre electricity demand could more than double by 2030. World Bank materials still describe Nigeria as having the world's largest electricity-access deficit.

The hard part is not whether demand exists. The hard part is whether energy can actually be delivered where compute can use it.
