**Title:** Why Alberta Still Matters to the Flare-to-Compute Conversation
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
Want to sound serious about flare-to-compute? Start by knowing Alberta.

## Body
Alberta still produces most of Canada's natural gas.

The flare-to-compute category is not emerging from nowhere. It sits on top of jurisdictions that already think deeply about gas, conservation, and field economics.

First: Canadian and Alberta sources still describe Alberta as the country's dominant gas-producing province.

Second: That matters because strong gas jurisdictions create the technical and commercial instincts that portable compute operators inherit.

Third: NRG Bloom can speak credibly in Africa when it understands the operating logic already proven in North American gas basins.

The edge in Africa will not come from pretending the category is brand new. It will come from mastering the regions that already taught the category how to think.
