# Short Video Script
**Title:** If You Want Scale, You Have to Understand Texas
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
If your stranded-gas thesis has no view on Texas, it probably has no view on scale.

## Body
Texas produced record natural gas volumes in 2024, and the Permian drove most U.S. production growth.

Texas matters because the Permian is where associated-gas scale, power demand, and modular deployment economics collide in the open.

First: EIA data shows Texas and the Permian remain central to U.S. gas-growth dynamics.

Second: That scale explains why modular power and compute operators keep showing up in and around Texas-linked energy infrastructure.

Third: NRG Bloom should use Texas as a scale analog, then explain why African execution demands a different field operating model.

The real signal from Texas is not hype. It is that scale belongs to operators who move at the speed of the basin.

## Close
Know Texas for scale. Know Africa for execution. Win by understanding both.
