# Short Video Script
**Title:** The Bakken Was a Proving Ground, Not a Footnote
**Format:** 45-60 second talking-head or kinetic text

## Hook
If you ignore the Bakken, you miss one of the category's most important proving grounds.

## Body
North Dakota hit a 94% gross gas-capture rate in July 2024.

North Dakota matters because it combined associated-gas scale, hard capture targets, and real infrastructure build-out under pressure.

First: North Dakota's policy framework kept pushing gas capture higher while production stayed large.

Second: That is exactly the kind of environment where flare-to-compute solutions either prove themselves or get exposed.

Third: NRG Bloom should speak about African deployment with the same seriousness that Bakken operators brought to gas handling and utilization.

The lesson from North Dakota is not just that flare mitigation is possible. It is that policy pressure and infrastructure constraint can create the conditions for category winners.

## Close
North Dakota showed what it looks like when stranded-gas logic meets industrial pressure.
