## Notes on Unverified Claims

Per `content-intelligence/do-not-say-yet-2026-03-26.md`, content built from this keyword
set must NOT assert as settled fact: exact build-cost figures (e.g., "$47K"), the Heirs
Energies or Canurta partnership names, a specific host-community power allocation
percentage, an active Verra VM0029 carbon-credit methodology position, the "$22B/year"
Nigerian backup-generator spend figure, or the "211 data centers for 1.4B people" stat.
Where content touches flare-gas carbon impact, AI-client pipeline, or partnership status,
use the safer substitute pattern: verified operating proof (live site, PPA rate range from
public NGFCP/market data), market-structure facts (grid gap %, NGFCP scale), and
interpretation that doesn't overstate certainty.
