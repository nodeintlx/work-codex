# NRG Bloom Content Engine Trust Policy

The content engine separates voice from evidence.

NRG Bloom's voice can come from operator experience, field judgment, and direct execution.
Market data, sector sizing, regulatory counts, and industry trend claims should come from
external evidence with clear provenance.

## Evidence Hierarchy

1. Institutional primary
   Examples: regulators, multilaterals, official datasets, government releases
2. Trusted industry analysis
   Examples: established research firms, sector reports, reputable trade publications
3. Trusted media
   Examples: credible journalism with named sourcing and dated reporting
4. Industry voice
   Examples: notable founders, operators, analysts, and X accounts the company trusts

## Engine Rules

- Every verified fact needs at least 3 sources.
- `external` and `hybrid` facts need at least 2 authoritative external sources.
- Voice-only sourcing is rejected.
- X posts and industry voices are supporting context, not core proof.
- `internal` facts must still include at least 1 internal source.

## Recommended Use

- Use `internal` for NRG Bloom operating history, company experience, and field observations.
- Use `external` for market size, flaring data, regulatory developments, and industry counts.
- Use `hybrid` when combining NRG Bloom execution proof with public market context.
